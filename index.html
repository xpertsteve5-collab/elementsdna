How verification works

Every lot is tested before it's listed — not after you ask.

Purity claims are only as good as the data behind them. Each batch is sent to an independent, ISO-certified laboratory for analytical testing, and the results are published as a downloadable Certificate of Analysis tied to that specific lot number.

Step 01

Third-party HPLC/MS

Identity and purity are measured by high-performance liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry at an accredited lab with no ownership stake in the result.

Step 02

Lot-level documentation

Results are recorded against the specific manufacturing lot — not a representative sample — so the CoA you download matches the vial you receive.

Step 03

Published before sale

CoA data is attached to the product listing ahead of shipment, so records are available for your files before you place an order.

Frequently asked questions

What does "research use only" mean here?

Products are supplied for in-vitro laboratory and analytical research by qualified researchers and institutions. They are not approved as drugs or supplements and are not sold, labeled, or intended for human or animal consumption or any clinical or diagnostic use.

How is purity verified?

Each lot is analyzed by an independent, ISO-certified laboratory using HPLC/MS methods. The resulting Certificate of Analysis is published against that lot number and made available for download.

Can I request a CoA for a specific order?

Yes. Every order is linked to its lot's Certificate of Analysis, retrievable from your account or by contacting support with your order number.

Where does testing take place?

Analytical testing is performed by accredited third-party laboratories based in the United States, independent of the supplier.
